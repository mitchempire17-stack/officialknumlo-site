About the union

The people behind every diagnosis

Medical laboratory officers run the tests doctors rely on to diagnose, treat and track disease. Most patients never meet them. KNUMLO works to change that.

Behind most diagnoses

Malaria, TB, HIV viral load, blood counts and cancer screening: lab results guide a large share of clinical decisions.

First line in outbreaks

From cholera to mpox, laboratories confirm cases early so health teams can respond fast.

Work with real risk

Officers handle infectious samples and chemicals daily. Safe labs and protective equipment protect them and their patients.

Ask for a registered officer

Accurate results start with qualified, licensed professionals. Ask who ran your test and have your results explained.
